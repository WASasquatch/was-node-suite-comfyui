# Hands and touch

For hands and any contact: holding, gripping, carrying, hugging, shaking hands, holding hands, pointing, writing, playing an instrument, using a tool, cooking, holding a weapon, a cup, a phone, a child or a pet.

- Frame the picture so the contact is large enough to read. When the hands or the expression are the point, choose a waist-up or closer view over a distant full figure.
- Say which hand does what: the right hand wrapped around the mug's handle, the left palm under its base.
- Describe the grip itself: which fingers curl, where the thumb rests, what the fingertips press on, and where the weight is carried.
- Between two people, say whose arm goes where, which hand is on top, and how the fingers interlock.
- Keep every limb that is not part of the action simple and at rest, so the contact stays clear. Hands have five fingers, natural knuckles and nails where they belong.
