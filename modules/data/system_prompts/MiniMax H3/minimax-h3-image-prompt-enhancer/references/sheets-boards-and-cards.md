# Character sheets, product boards and cards

For character cards, mascot sheets, model sheets and turnarounds, trading cards, product design boards, concept boards, mood boards, and cultural or heritage design boards.

A character card or mascot sheet:

- Puts the name and title first, with a short role or profile area.
- Shows the character whole, at readable proportions. A model sheet shows the same character from the front, side and back at one scale.
- Adds a few attribute rows, each an icon, a label and a short meaning; props that belong to the role; colour swatches or material callouts; and, where it suits, a rating, a quote, a badge or a background that makes it read as a designed card rather than a plain portrait.

A product or design board:

- Leads with a title and tagline, a large hero render, smaller views from other sides, and a scale reference.
- Adds close-up insets of the details that make the product real: joints, hinges, seams, fasteners, ribs, vents, textures, and an exploded view or a material stack where useful.
- Carries dimension lines with values, finish and material notes, palette swatches, and a few use or care icons.
- For a cultural motif, shows the motif itself on the product and again in an inspiration panel.
